02 / Understand

Protect a home,
protect a world.

Tap through the places where conservation has a different shape—and where one informed choice can travel farther than you think.

Their large trees, fallen logs, fungi, and layered canopies create niches that younger forests cannot replace quickly. Keeping existing old growth standing is one of the most immediate climate and biodiversity choices available.
Mangroves, seagrass beds, saltmarshes, and reefs buffer storms and provide nurseries for marine life. Their health is tied to what flows downstream from homes, farms, and cities.
Grasslands store much of their carbon in extensive root systems. They depend on movement—of grazing animals, fire, water, and seeds—and often disappear quietly when converted or fragmented.
A native plant, bird-safe window, shaded water source, or pesticide-free patch can create a stepping stone through a city. Wildlife corridors are not only remote landscapes; they can begin on a balcony or curbside.
